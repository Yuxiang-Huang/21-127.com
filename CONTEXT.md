# 21-127

The unofficial site for CMU Concepts of Mathematics, where a person can declare that they want the course.

## Language

**21-127**:
CMU Concepts of Mathematics. The single offering this site is about.

**Visitor**:
A person with no Clerk session.
_Avoid_: guest, anonymous user

**User**:
A person with a Clerk session. Uniqueness is the Clerk identity, not a human: two emails are two Users.
_Avoid_: account, student, member

**Interest**:
A User's current declaration that they want 21-127. It can be withdrawn.
_Avoid_: waitlist, click, like, vote, signup, RSVP

**Interest count**:
How many Interests exist right now.
_Avoid_: waitlist size, votes, signups
