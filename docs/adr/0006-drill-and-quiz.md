# Drill and Quiz are finite scored passes

A Topic's recall is two passes over Definitions, Techniques, and Theorems, and not over Problems. A Drill is the pass for one Subtopic, placed after Theorems and before Problems. A Quiz is the last Subtopic of the Topic and holds no kinds of its own. Each question is an authored list of parts plus a map from part index to wrong answers, so a later copy of a symbol can be a blank while an earlier copy stays visible. Easy through Mastery hide a fraction of those indexes and offer the part text among the wrong answers. Yuxiang hides the statement and scores typed LaTeX by the cosine similarity of OpenRouter embeddings.

## Considered Options

- An infinite matching of Definitions to statements. Rejected: a pass is one shuffle and a score, then it can be started again.
- Inferring atoms from the notes. Rejected: the author chooses which copy is a blank and which strings are the wrong answers.
- Scoring Yuxiang by comparing atoms. Rejected: Yuxiang is the similarity of the typed LaTeX to the joined parts.
